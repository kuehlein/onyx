# Politeness levels: 존댓말 vs 반말

Korean encodes how much respect you owe the listener directly in the verb
ending. Choosing the wrong level sounds either rude or oddly distant.

## Why it matters

존댓말 (jondaenmal, polite) is the safe default with strangers, elders, and
service staff. 반말 (banmal, casual) is for close friends and people clearly
younger than you — and only once it's mutually okay. When in doubt, use 존댓말.

## Example

Two forms of "give me": 주세요 (juseyo, polite) to a café server versus 줘 (jwo,
casual) to a close friend. The 물 주세요 you learn in this deck is polite on
purpose — it's what you'd actually say when ordering.
