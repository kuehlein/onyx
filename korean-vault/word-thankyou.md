# 감사합니다 (gamsahamnida)

A polite "thank you".

## Meaning

"Thank you" (formal/polite).

## Usage

Say it to a server, a stranger, or anyone you want to thank politely. 고마워요
(gomawoyo) is a softer, less formal alternative.
