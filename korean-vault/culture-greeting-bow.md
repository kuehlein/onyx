# Greeting etiquette: the bow

A small bow accompanies 안녕하세요 (annyeonghaseyo, "hello"). Its depth signals
respect — a light nod among peers, a deeper bow for elders or formal settings.

## Why it matters

A greeting with no bow at all can read as curt. You don't need a deep bow for a
café or shop — a small nod with 안녕하세요 is warm and correct.

## Example

Entering a shop: a light nod and 안녕하세요. Leaving after a purchase: 감사합니다
(gamsahamnida, "thank you") with another small nod.
