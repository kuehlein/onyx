# 주세요 (juseyo)

"Please give (me)" — the go-to way to ask for something.

## Meaning

"Please give me ___". Attach it after a noun.

## Usage

물 주세요 (mul juseyo) = "water, please". 이거 주세요 (igeo juseyo) = "this one,
please".
