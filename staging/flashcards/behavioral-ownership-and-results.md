# Ownership & Delivering Results

End-to-end accountability for an outcome — and shipping a measurable one.
Interviewers listen for a clear personal decision you drove, a hard result
(latency, cost, %, adoption), and follow-through past your formal scope. This is
the most universally weighted competency (Amazon: Ownership, Deliver Results,
Bias for Action).

## Prompts
- Tell me about a project you owned end-to-end. What was the outcome?
- Describe a time you delivered a result under a hard deadline or constraint.
- Tell me about something you took on that was outside your formal responsibility.
- Describe a time you had to make a tough call to hit a goal — what did you trade off?
- Tell me about an impact you're proud of. How did you measure it?
- A project you were on was slipping. What did you do?

## What strong looks like
Clear personal ownership — "I decided / I drove", roughly 80% "I" not "we", even
within a team. A **quantified** result and how it was measured, plus follow-through
beyond the ticket (owned the rollout, the on-call, the metric afterwards). Names the
tradeoff made and why. Backend flavor: owning a service, a migration, a reliability
or cost win with a real number. **Senior** = team-level, end-to-end. **Staff** =
cross-team/org-level result, set the direction others executed. Weak/penalized:
collective "we" with no personal decision, no metric, single-sprint scope,
hand-waved outcome, credit-claiming for others' work.
