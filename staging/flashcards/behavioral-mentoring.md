# Mentoring & Developing Others

Making the people and the team around you better. Interviewers look for concrete
investment in someone's growth, the method you used, and the outcome for them (not
just for you) — Amazon: Hire and Develop the Best; Meta: empathy.

## Prompts
- Tell me about someone you mentored. How did they grow?
- Describe a time you helped a struggling teammate turn things around.
- Tell me about raising the bar on your team (a practice, a standard, quality).
- Describe onboarding someone or bringing a junior up to speed.
- How have you given difficult feedback that helped someone improve?
- Tell me about delegating something important to develop someone.

## What strong looks like
Specific, sustained investment in a named person or the team: understood where they
were, chose an approach (pairing, stretch work, honest feedback, sponsorship), and
a concrete result *for them* (grew, shipped, got promoted). Empathy plus candor.
Backend flavor: leveling up on-call/design skills, establishing a review or testing
standard that lifted the whole team. **Senior** = mentored individuals. **Staff** =
scaled others via mechanisms (guilds, docs, sponsorship) and grew the team's ceiling.
Weak/penalized: "I answered their questions", took credit for their growth, vague
"I'm a great mentor" with no example or outcome.
