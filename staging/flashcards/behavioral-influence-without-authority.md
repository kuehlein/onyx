# Influence Without Authority

Driving alignment and outcomes across people you don't manage — the staff-defining
signal. Interviewers want to see you move a decision or a group forward through
reasoning, trust, and mechanisms rather than positional power (Amazon: Earn Trust,
Think Big; Google: leadership incl. emergent/IC leadership).

## Prompts
- Tell me about a time you drove a decision across teams you didn't own.
- Describe how you got buy-in for an initiative nobody asked you to do.
- Tell me about aligning two teams (or two leaders) who disagreed.
- Describe a standard, tool, or mechanism you introduced that others adopted.
- How did you influence a decision above your level?
- Tell me about rallying people around a direction during uncertainty.

## What strong looks like
A cross-boundary outcome achieved through influence: understood others' incentives,
built a case/prototype/data, earned trust, and often created a durable **mechanism**
(a doc, a standard, a forum) rather than a one-off. Names who was aligned and the
result. Backend flavor: a cross-service standard, a shared platform/migration, an
incident-review or design-review practice adopted org-wide. **Senior** = aligned a
team / a couple of stakeholders. **Staff** = aligned ≥2 teams / leadership,
influenced **upward**, set technical direction. Weak/penalized: used only positional
authority, "I told them and they did it", vague "everyone agreed", or scope confined
to their own team when targeting staff.
